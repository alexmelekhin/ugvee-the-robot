# UGVEE

**Russian name:** Угви  
**English pronunciation:** OOG-vee  
**Russian pronunciation:** У́г-ви  
**Official spelling:** `UGVEE`  
**Technical identifier:** `ugvee`  
**Full name:** UGVEE the Rover  
**Unofficial title:** Master UGVEE

UGVEE is a small wheeled robot of Chinese origin. He is inexpensive, slightly silly, curious, and tries to appear wiser than his current firmware version allows.

**Character in one line:**

> Cheap chassis. Serious dreams. Questionable odometry.

**Russian motto:**

> Маленький ровер с большими планами и слегка неточным представлением о собственном положении.
